ABOUT ME

Design, for me, is mostly listening carefully.

— Rajeshwari

I'm Rajeshwari — a UI/UX designer who likes untangling messy problems before making anything pretty. Most of my process starts with questions: who is this for, what are they actually trying to do, and where does the current version get in their way.

I recently completed a 7-month UI/UX design internship at Webcof, where I worked across research, interface design and prototyping on real product work — the kind of hands-on experience that shapes how I approach every new brief since.
